FAQ

Before you join

How many tips do I get each week?

Free gives you one tip per matchday. VIP covers the full weekend slate with 5–8 tips plus a banker and an accumulator. Premium adds daily and midweek fixtures on top of that.

How do I receive the tips?

All tips are posted in our Telegram channel ahead of kickoff, along with the reasoning behind them. VIP and Premium tips are unlocked once your weekly payment is confirmed.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes — plans bill weekly rather than locking you into a monthly or annual contract, so you can stop, pause, or switch tiers before the next cycle starts.

Are wins guaranteed?

No. Tips are informed analysis, not certainties — football is unpredictable, and no tipster can guarantee a result. Always bet within your means.
